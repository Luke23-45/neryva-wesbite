# Company Page Data Plan

## Objective

Define the Company page content before implementation so the page can be rebuilt without using the legacy lab-focused dataset.

## Final Content Model

The Company page should use page-level metadata plus section-level JSON files.

Recommended files:

- `page.json`
- `sections/hero.json`
- `sections/mission.json`
- `sections/values.json`
- `sections/team.json`
- `sections/contact_cta.json`

## Section Responsibilities

### Hero

Purpose:

- state the company identity clearly,
- establish the trust surface quickly.

Content requirements:

- label,
- headline,
- short description,
- visual note.

### Mission

Purpose:

- explain why the company exists,
- connect the identity to the research-led enterprise foundation.

Content requirements:

- mission statement,
- compact rationale,
- clear alignment with the company foundation.

### Values

Purpose:

- state the operating principles,
- keep the company honest about scope and evidence.

Content requirements:

- concise values,
- no inflated language,
- no aspirational filler without meaning.

### Team

Purpose:

- show who is involved or how the team is currently structured,
- be explicit when details are not finalized.

Content requirements:

- current status,
- roles or placeholders if necessary,
- no fabricated biographies.

### Contact CTA

Purpose:

- close the page with a direct action,
- send the visitor to the contact route or relevant next step.

Content requirements:

- short message,
- contact route,
- optional partnership or hiring note.

## Content Standards

- Keep the page clear and human.
- Avoid turning the page into a marketing brochure.
- Avoid turning it into a recruitment page.
- Keep it honest about what is ready and what is still being assembled.

## Validation Criteria

Before implementation, the Company page data should satisfy all of the following:

- the page reads as identity and trust content,
- the mission is aligned with the foundation,
- the team section does not invent people,
- the values are grounded,
- the page can be implemented without future rework from hidden assumptions.
