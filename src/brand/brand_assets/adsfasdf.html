III
Concept III
The Meridian
Unity · Propagation · Synthesis

The definitive synthesis. This mark inherits the full circle and action potential waveform from V1, the distribution semicircle base from V2A's Horizon, and adds a new element: three concentric propagation rings below the baseline, representing signal emission and reach.

The baseline at y=50 is the meridian — the threshold between Known and Unknown. The semicircle below is the Known Distribution. The waveform breaches it. The apex dot hovers in the territory above all boundaries. Most narrative; most complete.

DNA From
V1 Circle + Waveform + V2A Horizon base. Plus new: propagation arcs.
Aesthetic
Scientific. Cosmological. Complete. Narrative-rich.
Geometry
Circular symmetry. Three distinct depth layers. Multiple readings.
Best Use
Primary brand mark. Conference materials. White papers. Keynote stages.

IV
Concept IV
The Axiom
Truth · Reduction · Essence

Radical reductionism. This mark contains no circle, no container, no enclosure of any kind. Just the waveform. But executed with extreme stroke weight contrast: the baseline segments are heavy (3px), while the spike itself is a hairline (0.8px).

The weight contrast is the meaning: the thick baseline is the world of the Known; the hairline spike is the signal that cannot be captured in conventional measurement — only glimpsed, at extreme precision. A mark of pure scientific philosophy.

DNA From
V1 Waveform, stripped to absolute essence.
Aesthetic
Brutally minimal. Haiku-like. Philosophical.
Geometry
Stroke-weight contrast as the sole compositional tool. Zero filled shapes.
Best Use
Data dashboards, watermarks, digital interfaces, embossed stationery.
